How to Start Affiliate Marketing With No Budget

You don't need to buy a course or run ads to learn the basics. Start small, check the rules, publish useful content, and measure what happens.

By Fluxiq Labs · Beginner guide

Affiliate marketing is a business model where a publisher may earn a commission when someone purchases through a tracked referral link. Earnings depend on the programme, the audience, the offer, and whether a referred purchase qualifies.

1. Choose one audience and one problem

Instead of promoting everything, start with a specific audience and useful topic. For example, help beginners learn mobile video editing or understand digital products.

2. Check the programme before promoting it

  • Confirm minimum age and eligibility rules.
  • Read commission, refund, and payout terms.
  • Check whether your country and traffic sources are accepted.
  • Never claim a product guarantees income or results without proper evidence.

3. Publish helpful content for free

Use a free website or blog to answer specific questions. Tutorials, checklists, comparisons, and transparent reviews can help readers make informed decisions. Don't copy other creators or pretend you've personally tested something you haven't.

4. Track the funnel

Record article visits, outbound clicks, qualifying sales, and confirmed commissions. Views alone are not income. If a page gets views but few clicks, improve its relevance and calls to action. If people click but don't buy, review the offer, audience fit, and sales page.

5. Run a realistic 15-day test

Set aside 30–60 minutes a day. Publish a small number of high-quality articles, share them only where permitted, and check results. Fifteen days is enough to test your workflow, but not enough to guarantee search traffic or sales.

Keep it honest: disclose affiliate relationships clearly, protect readers' privacy, and don't spam links or invent earnings screenshots. If you're under the programme's minimum age, don't register using false information; ask whether a permitted guardian-managed arrangement exists.
